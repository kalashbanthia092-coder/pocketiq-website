About PocketIQ

PocketIQ is an AI-powered financial literacy platform built to help teenagers and young adults make smarter money decisions in the real world.

Traditional financial education is often abstract, jargon-heavy, or unrealistic from everyday decisions. PocketIQ bridges this gap by combining interactive learning, decision guidance, and visual comparisons in a versatile and accessible interface.

About Me

Hi! I’m Kalash, a highschool student passionate about making finance practical, relatable, and engaging for young people. PocketIQ is my attempt to turn financial literacy from theory into skills you can actually use throughout your financial journey.

This project combines AI, UX design, and real-world scenarios to help users learn at their own pace and make confident financial choices.
