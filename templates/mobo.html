How this goes

  1. Tell MoBo the item and the price.
  2. Answer a few questions about why you want it.
  3. Get a verdict you can act on.

What it weighs up

  • The price against what you actually have
  • How often you'd genuinely use it
  • Whether it replaces something you own
  • What else that money could do
💡 Rule of thumb

Sleep on anything you didn't plan to buy. Most urges don't survive 24 hours.
